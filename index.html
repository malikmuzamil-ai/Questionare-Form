02

What Only You Know

4 questions

These four carry the most weight in the whole form. Your signature pillar gets built out of them, and it is the one part of your strategy a competitor could not publish.

Q5
The thing you would argue about with a peer who knows their stuff. Not the safe version you would say on a podcast.
Posting more is not the fix. Every founder I have worked with under 20k a month was already posting enough. They were posting about the wrong problem, and adding volume just made it louder. Notice it names who it applies to and what it argues against.
Q6
Step by step, in the order you do it. Include the boring first step, because that is usually the one nobody else talks about and it becomes some of your best content.
Q7
Client results, how long clients stay, years doing this, how many you have done. If the honest answer is none yet, write that. The plan changes to build proof over the 30 days instead of claiming it.
Q8
Their words, not your summary. The reasons that come up more than once. Week three of your plan is built entirely from this answer.
"I need to think about it." "This only works if you already have an audience." "I don't have time to post every day." "Let me get through this quarter first." Four sentences you have heard before is a better answer than one paragraph explaining them.
